# Referral Program

We're excited to announce Polymarket's referral program. To qualify, your Polymarket account must achieve at least $10,000 in lifetime trading volume. Get paid to refer users to Polymarket, and be eligible for future Polymarket rewards.

## **​Referral Link**

Share any market or profile page using the share button on that page. When someone clicks your link and signs up, they become your referral. You can share links before you meet the eligibility requirements, but you only start earning once your account reaches $10,000 in lifetime trading volume. To count, a new user must sign up within **30 days** of clicking your link.

The more that users you refer trade, the more you'll earn — subject to the caps below.

## **​Rewards**

You'll earn:

- 10% of net fees generated from direct referrals

- 5% of net fees generated from indirect referrals

Direct referrals are those you personally refer, and indirect referrals are those referred by someone you've referred (i.e. if you refer Alice and Alice refers Bob, you'll earn **5%** of net trading fees generated from Bob's trading volume as well).

These rewards apply for the first **30 days** after a user signs up, or until they reach the **Platinum** tier (whichever comes first). Rewards apply through every tier up to and including **Gold**. This period and these terms are subject to change without notice.

## **​Notes**

- You will earn referral rewards based on the **net** trading fee that a user generates on Polymarket. Net fees are what Polymarket keeps after the referred user's own tier rebate.

- Not all polymarkets have fees, so you won't make $ from volume on every polymarket.

- Rewards are paid out once a day at midnight UTC in **pUSD**.

- The more the users you refer trade on the platform, the more you can earn, subject to the caps above.

- Polymarket reserves the right, in its sole discretion, to disqualify referrals and withhold or claw back rewards that violate our Terms of Service — including but not limited to self-referrals, **linked accounts**, or inauthentic trading.
