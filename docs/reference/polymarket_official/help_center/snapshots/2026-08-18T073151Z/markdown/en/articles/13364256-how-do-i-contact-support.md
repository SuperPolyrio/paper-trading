# How Do I Contact Support?

Polymarket offers technical support through our website chat feature, and via mail.

To contact support through our website:

- Navigate to **[Polymarket](https://polymarket.com/)**.

- Click the blue chat icon in the bottom right and start your chat session.

For support via mail:

- Contact: <support@polymarket.com> for the website

- Contact: <support@polymarket.us> for the US app
