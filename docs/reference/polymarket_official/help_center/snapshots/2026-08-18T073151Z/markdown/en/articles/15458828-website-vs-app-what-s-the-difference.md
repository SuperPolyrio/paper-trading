# Website vs App - What's the Difference?

# **Overview**

Polymarket is available in different versions depending on where you are. Polymarket.com is for international users, while US residents must use the Polymarket US app. These are separate products with separate support teams.

## **Polymarket.com (Web) - International Users**

Polymarket.com is available to users outside the United States, with exception of [restricted countries](https://help.polymarket.com/en/articles/13364163-geographic-restrictions). It's accessible on any device with a modern browser - desktop, tablet, or mobile.

- No installation required - just visit polymarket.com

- For support, contact <support@polymarket.com>

**Please note:** Polymarket.com is not accessible for US residents. US users cannot deposit funds or place trades on the website.

​

## **Polymarket US iOS App - US Users Only**

The Polymarket US app is a separate product designed specifically for users in the United States. It is available on the Apple App Store for iPhone.

- Entirely separate from Polymarket.com - accounts are not connected

- For support, use the in-app chat or contact <support@polymarket.us>
